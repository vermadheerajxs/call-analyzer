# Customer journey

## Household customers

People needing to store belongings while traveling, renovating, between moves, or for temporary space constraints. They ask about duration, monthly cost, safety, pickup, and city/facility.

## Relocating customers

People shifting home or office. Journey often: enquiry → quotation → negotiation → booking → pickup → transit → delivery. Follow-ups may skip full rediscovery if prior context exists.

## Temporary vs long-term storage

Short-term (days/weeks) and long-term (months) storage are both valid. Duration strongly affects pricing discussions. Listen for "kuch din", "1 month", "3 months", "6 months", etc.

## Business / inventory customers

Companies needing warehouse space, stock storage, or recurring logistics. Discovery focuses on volume, access, duration, location, and commercial terms rather than sofa/furniture household details.

## Existing customers

Already booked or previously served. Calls may concern pickup timing, delays, access to stored goods, delivery, billing, or complaints. Do not treat them as brand-new cold leads unless the transcript shows a new enquiry.

## Lead sources (context only)

Calls may come from ads, app, website, referrals, or callbacks. Source does not change disposition rules; it only explains why the call started.
