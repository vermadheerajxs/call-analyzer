# Support and operations

## Existing-customer support

If the customer refers to a prior booking, promised pickup, stored goods, or "you said you would call back", treat as support/operations context unless they clearly start a brand-new sales enquiry.

## Delayed or missed pickup

Example: "mera pickup kal hona tha, abhi tak koi nahi aaya". This is typically an operational/support/logistics issue for an existing commitment, not a new household-storage sales lead.

Good handling: acknowledge delay, take ownership, check status, give concrete next step or escalation, avoid empty promises.

## Complaints

Evaluate empathy, understanding, ownership, communication, resolution attempt, escalation, and next steps. Do not score the agent down merely because no new sale occurred.

## Callbacks already promised

If the customer reminds the agent of a prior callback commitment, the call is often follow-up/support. Disposition may be Callback Requested or Follow Up Required based on what is agreed next.
