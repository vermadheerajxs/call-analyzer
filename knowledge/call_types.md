# Call types

## New lead / enquiry / advertisement lead

First contact about storage or moving. Customer explores need, city, duration, price. Typical dispositions: Interested, Not Interested, Follow Up Required, Callback Requested, Closed Won/Lost.

## Sales / quotation

Focused on rates, packages, space size, packing charges. Outcome often follow-up, callback, or booking.

## Follow-up

Continuation of a prior conversation. Agent should not be penalized for skipping full initial discovery. Look for progress: revised quote, decision, objection, booking.

## Callback

Customer or agent agreed to call again at a time. Prefer disposition Callback Requested when a callback is the clear outcome.

## Booking / Closed Won style outcome

Customer agrees to proceed/book. Transcript must support commitment; do not assume booking from interest alone.

## Existing customer / support

Operational call about an existing booking or stored goods. Not primarily a sales pitch call.

## Complaint / logistics issue

Problems such as missed pickup, late vehicle, damaged goods, poor communication. Evaluate handling: understanding, ownership, next steps, escalation—not whether a sale was closed.

## Post-service

After move/storage: feedback, remaining items, billing clarification, retrieval of goods.

## B2B

Commercial warehouse/inventory/logistics discussion. Different discovery than household sofa storage.

## Wrong number / invalid / no conversation

Wrong person, silence, or no intelligible discussion. Prefer Wrong Number, No Answer, or Voicemail as appropriate.
