# B2B and business storage

## What B2B calls look like

Business users discuss warehouse space, stock/inventory storage, commercial duration, access frequency, and logistics—not sofa/furniture household details alone.

Example intent: "warehouse mein stock rakhna hai".

## How this differs from household storage

- Goods: inventory/stock vs household furniture
- Decision makers: managers/companies vs individuals/families
- Terms: commercial rates, SLAs, recurring pickup/delivery may appear
- Disposition still uses the same closed set; interpret outcome in commercial context

## Do not force B2B label

If the transcript is clearly household furniture storage, do not reclassify as B2B just because "godown" or "warehouse" was mentioned casually. Prefer the dominant customer need in the transcript.
