# Sales call context

## Goal of sales/enquiry calls

Understand need (storage vs shifting vs B2B), city/location, duration/volume, constraints, and move the customer toward a clear next step: quote, callback, follow-up, or booking.

## Good sales handling signals

- Clarifies storage vs relocation vs warehouse need
- Asks relevant discovery (city, duration, goods type, timing)
- Explains next steps clearly
- Schedules callback or sends quotation when customer needs time
- Remains polite under soft refusal

## What not to over-read

Interest alone is not Closed Won. "I'll think about it" is usually Follow Up Required or Interested, not a completed booking.

A polite "not interested" is Not Interested; that is not automatically Closed Lost unless an active deal was killed after consideration.

## Quotation calls

Customers comparing rates are normal. Capture outcome: accepted, rejected, pending discussion with family/manager, or callback.
