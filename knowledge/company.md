# Company

## What Xtended Space is

Xtended Space is an Indian storage and relocation partner. It helps customers store belongings and move homes or offices. The business combines storage facilities with packers-and-movers style logistics (pickup, packing, transport, delivery).

## Positioning

Xtended Space markets itself as a storage and relocation partner with verified storage properties, hosts/spaces across multiple cities, and app/web booking for storage and moving enquiries.

Public positioning highlights include large available space inventory, multi-city coverage, host/property listings, and customer ratings. Exact city coverage and promotions can change; never invent a city or discount that the transcript does not support.

## Who customers talk to on calls

Calls may involve sales/enquiry agents, follow-up agents, booking coordinators, or support/operations agents handling existing customers (pickup delays, storage access, delivery, complaints).

## What calls are usually about

Typical reasons someone calls Xtended Space:

- Need temporary or long-term storage for household goods
- Home or office relocation / shifting
- Packers and movers quotation
- Business/warehouse or inventory storage (B2B)
- Follow-up on a prior quotation or callback
- Existing booking: pickup, delivery, delay, complaint, change request
