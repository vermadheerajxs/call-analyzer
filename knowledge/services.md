# Services

## Household storage

Customers store furniture, boxes, appliances, and household goods for weeks or months. Common need: "ghar ka saman kuch mahine ke liye rakhna hai" (keep household items for some months). This is a household storage requirement, not necessarily relocation.

Signals: storage, store, rakhna, saman/samaan, furniture, sofa, temporary storage, long-term storage, months, godown (when personal goods).

## Packers and movers / home relocation

Customers moving homes need packing, loading, transport, and unloading. Common need: "ghar shift karna hai" / office shift. This is relocation / packers & movers, which may or may not include storage.

Signals: shift, shifting, relocate, relocation, packers, movers, packing, move house, ghar shift, office shift.

## Office relocation

Businesses moving offices need commercial packing and transport. Treat as relocation/logistics with commercial context, not a simple household storage enquiry.

## Business / B2B / warehouse storage

Businesses store inventory, stock, or goods in warehouse-style space. Common need: "warehouse mein stock rakhna hai". This is B2B/business storage, often different pricing and requirements than household storage.

Signals: warehouse, stock, inventory, B2B, commercial, business storage, godown for stock, recurring logistics.

## Pickup and delivery logistics

Xtended Space related logistics include pickup from customer location and delivery/drop to storage or new address. Delays, missed pickups, and reschedules are operational issues for existing bookings, not new sales leads by default.

Signals: pickup, pick up, delivery, drop, vehicle, driver, aaj aana tha, kal aana tha, late, delay.

## Quotation and pricing discussions

Sales calls often discuss rates, monthly rent, duration, city, space size, packing charges, and pickup/drop charges. "kitna lagega" / quotation / rate are sales enquiry signals.

Do not invent prices. Only use amounts stated in the transcript.
