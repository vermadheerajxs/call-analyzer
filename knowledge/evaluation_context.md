# Evaluation context for call analysis

## Priority of evidence

1. The current transcript is authoritative for what happened.
2. Application disposition/rating rules next.
3. Retrieved Xtended Space knowledge is supporting context only.
4. General model knowledge last.

Never invent prices, cities, bookings, or promises from knowledge that the transcript does not state.

## Rating is not only outcome

Customer refusal does not automatically mean poor agent performance. A professional, clear handling of a "no" can still deserve a mid/fair rating.

Successful booking does not automatically mean excellent performance; agent behavior still matters.

## Support vs sales

Support/operations calls should not be penalized for not pitching a new sale during a complaint or pickup delay.

Complaint/logistics calls: score understanding, empathy, ownership, clarity, resolution/next steps.

## Follow-up calls

Do not expect full first-call discovery again. Judge progress and clarity of next steps.

## Map intent before disposition

- Household goods for months → household storage context
- Ghar/office shift → relocation / packers & movers context
- Missed pickup on promised date → support/logistics context
- Warehouse stock → B2B storage context

Then choose disposition from the allowed list based on the actual outcome in the transcript.
